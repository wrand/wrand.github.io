About

Welcome. I am a faculty member and the Executive Director of the Institute for Advanced Analytics at NC State University in Raleigh, NC. My work focuses on the intersection of data science, artificial intelligence, and complex systems.

Research & Publications

My primary research interests include agent-based modeling, information diffusion, and social network analysis. I am currently finalizing the second edition of my co-authored textbook on Agent-Based Modeling.

Software

I serve as a core software developer and contributor to the NetLogo modeling environment, working via pull requests and issue tracking to maintain and expand the computational paradigm for researchers and educators worldwide.
