About

Welcome. I am a Professor of Management, Innovation, and Entrepreneurship at the NC State Poole College of Management and the Executive Director of the Institute for Advanced Analytics. My work explores the intersection of data science, artificial intelligence, and complex systems, building on my background at the University of Michigan.

Research Interests

My research leverages computational techniques to understand complex organizational and consumer behavior. Current areas of focus include:

  • Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) & Information Diffusion
  • Algorithmic Bias and the ethical deployment of AI
  • AI Attribution Effects on worker mental health and productivity
  • Visual Analytics and fMRI applications in business research
